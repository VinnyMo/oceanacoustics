# Recording geography and surface conditions

The worker publishes `/data/globe.json` from its SQLite ledger. Each playable recording links to its location and recording-hour context in `/data/curation/<id>.json`. The globe refreshes with the observatory snapshot. Current atmospheric and marine estimates come from Open-Meteo’s free noncommercial APIs. Displayed units and UTC validity times come from the provider. The values describe a model grid cell. The model can differ substantially from conditions near a coastal or deep hydrophone.

## Positions

The compact coordinate index in `research/positions.json` includes source links. MBARI uses the published MARS observatory node. Orcasound uses its public feed positions. PANGAEA uses the dataset point or the labeled center of its geographic extent. NOAA joins the final deployment identifier to a unique latitude/longitude pair in the NCEI SanctSound and NRS metadata tables. When a new SanctSound deployment is missing from the bulk table, the worker can resolve its deployment JSON and verify the embedded deployment name. Unresolved positions stay in the station list without a map pin or a model lookup.

## Current layers and archives

- The globe shows readings at recording stations and at 58 geographically distributed sample points, including 40 ocean points. Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons supply the public-domain coastline and ocean mask. Circles select stations. Squares show model samples. The displayed numbers use the provider's units. Wind and current arrows show direction of travel.
- Each cycle requests up to six station positions and six background positions per product. Successful station requests refresh after one hour and background requests after three hours. The map drops station values over two hours old and background values over four hours old. The station detail can still show a timestamped last reading.
- The worker also attempts up to six pending station/UTC-hour/product lookups per cycle. All clips at the same deployment and hour share the result. Older atmospheric records use ERA5. Before 2022, the marine request uses ERA5-Ocean wave height. From 2022 the marine endpoint can also supply currents and sea-surface temperature where available. The provider supplies model values at the hour containing the recording. Precipitation describes an accumulation interval. Wave height and surface currents describe different processes.
- Requests retain the queried URL, model selection, grid coordinates, valid time, retrieval time, units and available values. A missing result stays missing. Each network failure backs off for 30 minutes. Historical values are reused across cycles and restarts. Refresh and backfill run in the existing unprivileged worker under the existing quota.
- Pip receives recording-hour model evidence, including grid coordinates and units. The request’s environmental inputs are saved in SQLite with the listening attempt in `ai_environment`. Earlier notes retain their original interpretation. Historical matching does not trigger a new paid review.
- The Research inventory counts MBARI processing windows separately from other archives’ screened excerpts, with native rate, encoding or work mode, processed duration, metered audio bytes and the earliest and latest recording start times. Transfer counters vary by adapter. MBARI counts analyzed ranges, PANGAEA counts the PCM range, NOAA counts acquired FLAC bytes and Orcasound counts the transport segment when metered. Totals exclude metadata transfer and show partial or missing measurements for older excerpts.
