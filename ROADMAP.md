# Roadmap

- Activate the bounded service on the VPS; check origin, public TLS, certificate renewal, recovery after reboot, and multi-day resource receipts.
- Inventory effort by deployment and time, reconcile all-zero intervals, recorder changes, source revisions, duty cycles and unavailable days.
- Establish blinded held-out positive and negative controls, measure performance by noise stratum and evaluate tentative groups before changing thresholds.
- Extend review workflows and calibrated reference comparisons. Evaluate sustained-chorus detectors alongside the short-event baseline.
- Add ONC archive support after checking credentials, deployment-specific licensing and publication delays. Extend multi-site comparisons only with compatible timing and instruments.
- Prototype signed, replayable work units, checksum verification and independent reproducibility checks before accepting external CPU or AI-agent results.
